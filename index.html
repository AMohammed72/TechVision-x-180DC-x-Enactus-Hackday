⚠️ CONTROLLABLE PROFIT DRAIN IDENTIFIED: THIRD-PARTY DELIVERY PLATFORMS

While delivery generates £1.53M in sales (18.4% of total), it accounts for £453.6K in commission fees, £47.6K in packaging, and £98.8K in staff packing time (~4 min/order). This crushes delivery's gross contribution margin to 31.7% (vs 72.5% for In-Store and App Click & Collect) and yields a net negative margin of -2.8% after store overhead allocation.

🧩 Architecture Modularity: How Dynamic Ingestion Works

Every section of this application is decoupled and reactive. If a user appends a new event row to business_events.csv (e.g. Summer Menu Launch, 2026-05-01, Introduced cold brew range) or drops an updated CSV into Tab 7:

  • Auto-Event Detection: The dynamic pipeline parses the event date, maps it to its timeline window, and calculates pre- vs post-event financial shifts automatically.
  • Trajectory Integration: The chart renderer draws a new dashed vertical event line and flag on the Canvas timeline.
  • Store-Specific Filtering: The engine matches store mentions in the event description (e.g. Leeds Briggate, Trinity, Sheffield) or applies it chain-wide.
