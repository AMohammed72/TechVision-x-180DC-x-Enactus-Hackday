⚠️ CONTROLLABLE PROFIT DRAIN IDENTIFIED: THIRD-PARTY DELIVERY PLATFORMS

While delivery generates £1.53M in sales (18.4% of total), it accounts for £453.6K in commission fees, £47.6K in packaging, and £98.8K in staff packing time (~4 min/order). This crushes delivery's gross contribution margin to 31.7% (vs 72.5% for In-Store and App Click & Collect) and yields a net negative margin of -2.8% after store overhead allocation.

Controllable Action 1: Enforce £12+ minimum basket size on Dashly & RushEats Controllable Action 2: Pass £0.50 packaging fee to delivery customers Controllable Action 3: Migrate delivery customers to App Click & Collect (0% commission)

💡 HUFF GRAVITY STRATEGIC INSIGHT: WHY LEEDS WELLINGTON PLACE (CS7) IS A CANNIBALIZATION TRAP

In stakeholder interviews, leadership noted looking at Leeds Wellington Place (CS7) and Sheffield Kelham Island (CS2). While CS7 generates an estimated Standalone Profit of £4,137/mo (8,947 customers/mo @ £6.43 avg spend), it sits just 0.42 miles from S12 (Leeds Trinity) and 0.52 miles from S03 (Leeds Briggate). The Huff Gravity Model shows CS7 diverts -£5,301/mo in profit from S12 and S03, yielding a Net Chain Impact of -£1,165/mo! Conversely, CS1 (Leeds Headingley, 2.5 mi away), CS5 (Liverpool Bold Street, Greenfield), and CS2 (Sheffield Kelham Island, 1.25 mi away) deliver strong net-positive chain profit.
